What you need to believe

Three things have to be true. We think they are.

1

Doctors won't change

The only way to digitise Indian healthcare is technology that adapts to paper — not the other way around. Every product that asked doctors to type has failed here.

2

ABDM is a platform shift

A once-in-a-generation regulatory moment. Whoever wins the institutions wins the decade — which is why we built on ABDM's data model from the first schema, not as a later retrofit.

3

Mobile-first is the only way in

Doctors, pharmacists and lab technicians have phones, not laptops. Meet them where they are — Android-first, offline-first.

In-patient treatment charts

The hardest page in the ward.

Multi-day, multi-author charts — a week of dosing rounds and nursing entries on one dense sheet. Most clinical AI won't attempt it. Santhica reads it into a structured medication schedule.

  • ✓ Multi-day dosing grids
  • ✓ Low-confidence rows flagged for review, never guessed
  • ✓ Designed around in-patient wards, not out-patient forms

Clinical safety net

The result nobody chased is the one that hurts.

Santhica watches for abnormal results and for tests that were ordered but never came back — opens a case, ranks it by severity, and escalates until someone acts. Follow-up stops depending on memory.

  • ✓ Abnormal results detected and ranked
  • ✓ Ordered-but-pending tests tracked automatically
  • ✓ Escalation ladder with a full audit trail
  • ✓ Allergy and interaction checks before saving
Longitudinal record

One patient. One record. Every visit.

Every prescription, chart and consultation joins a single timeline that belongs to the patient — not to the clinic that happened to create it. Visit another Santhica clinic and the history travels with them, on their consent.

  • ✓ Active medications and conditions at a glance
  • ✓ Cross-clinic access on patient-granted, revocable consent
  • ✓ Every access logged
  • ✓ Multi-doctor clinics with roles and invitations

Built for real clinics

Designed for the conditions Indian practice actually runs in.

Patchy networks, shared devices, paper workflows — and a regulator that expects the record to belong to the patient.

📶

Works offline

Register patients, capture prescriptions and record visits with no connection at all. Everything syncs when the network returns.

🔒

Encrypted on device

The local database is encrypted and biometric-locked. Patient data is wiped on sign-out and when switching clinics.

🪪

ABDM-architected

Patient identity, consent and care contexts are core tables from the first schema — not a compliance layer bolted on afterwards.

📱

One device

The phone the doctor already owns. No desktop, no server in the back room, no IT visit.

👥

Multi-doctor clinics

Invite colleagues, assign roles, switch between clinics — with data scoped so nothing leaks across organisations.

📤

Your data, exportable

Import an existing patient list, export everything, any time. No lock-in by hostage-taking.
